Tuning
Click the sidebar or a tile. Alt+↓/↑ next or previous workspace (hold it to switch fast). Alt+O overview, Esc back. In the overview: arrows move, Enter opens, wheel pans. Click a group heading (or g) to zoom to that group, Esc back out. Drag a row, or use its arrows, to slide it. Typing goes to the current workspace's terminal, also mid-flight.
